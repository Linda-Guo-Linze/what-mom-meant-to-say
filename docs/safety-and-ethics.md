# Safety and ethics

## Boundary

The app offers one possible interpretation. It cannot know verified thoughts and does not diagnose, prescribe, change medication or dosage, recommend restraint, or provide emergency treatment.

## Deterministic safety route

Self-harm, harm, weapons, inability to wake, breathing difficulty, wandering, traffic risk, or sudden severe change bypass ordinary Live AI generation. Urgent and emergency results use fixed human-help steps and disable speech.

## Privacy

Profiles, photos, preferences, and history stay in browser IndexedDB unless the user deliberately submits a scene in Live mode. Photos are never submitted. Before a Live request, common contact, address, government ID, and medical ID patterns are replaced and profile names are neutralized. This is data minimization, not guaranteed de-identification. Responses are no-store and credentials remain server-only.

## Speech

Speech uses an installed English browser/system voice after a user click. It is not a recording, voice clone, or imitation of a specific person. The full transcript remains visible, playback can be stopped, and elevated-risk output cannot be spoken.

## Limits

Live AI is limited at browser, IP, and site levels and uses one call without automatic model retry. Timeouts, provider errors, invalid JSON, unsupported evidence, or prohibited output fall back to Stable Demo.
